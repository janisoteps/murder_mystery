# Season 2 ground truth — *The Ninth Bell*

> GAME-MASTER EYES ONLY. This file contains the immutable solution. Never serve it to the browser, quote it to players, or use its wording in image-generation instructions.

## The public crime

At 20:18 on Thursday 14 November 2004, Dr. Annika Ravn is found dead beneath the decommissioned storm bell inside Saint Vigga Sea Fort on Vesperholm, a fictional Danish island in the Kattegat. Her coat and hair are wet, yet the bell chamber is dry and locked. A blue glass splinter lies in her cuff. The fort clock has stopped at 19:41.

Annika did not die at the fort. She was sedated at Gull House Hotel, abducted through its service court, taken in a North Channel Ferries maintenance van to the Tidegate Pumping Station, and deliberately drowned in the rainwater settling cistern by Freja Moller. Her body was then carried by Freja's salvage skiff to the fort's submerged service landing. Oskar Lind raised it through the bell-maintenance lift and staged the chamber. Nils Rask planned the abduction and ordered the cover-up. Dr. Emil Jessen knowingly supplied and administered the sedative, although he believed Nils intended coercion rather than murder.

The conspiracy has four differently culpable members:

- **Nils Rask — mastermind.** He planned Annika's abduction to recover the original *Eidolon* bridge recorder and prevent a reopened maritime inquiry. He authorized force, arranged the van, access, camera outage, and payment. After learning that Freja had killed Annika, he ordered Oskar to stage the body and destroy the recorder. He did not deliver the fatal act, but the murder followed directly from his plan and he knowingly concealed it.
- **Freja Moller — physical murderer and transport.** She drove the van, guarded Annika at Tidegate, and drowned her deliberately when Annika threatened to expose Freja's most damning act aboard the *Eidolon*. She transported the body by skiff and later burned her gloves.
- **Dr. Emil Jessen — knowing abduction accomplice.** He diverted hospital midazolam, injected Annika in the Gull House service corridor, falsified the drug register, and examined her at Tidegate. He left before the killing and believed she was alive when he departed. Afterward he wrote a false emergency call into the clinic log and kept silent.
- **Oskar Lind — after-the-fact stager with prior knowledge of the abduction.** He supplied a Tidegate contractor card and a duplicate fort key, believing the plan was intimidation. After the killing, he received the body at the fort, used the maintenance lift, planted blue museum glass, stopped the clock, and locked the chamber. He later hid the flood sling and the fort lift crank.

Jesper Holm, the ferry dispatcher, disabled two cameras after Nils told him it was a union inspection. He did not know about the abduction and is not a conspirator. Solveig Brandt, the island constable, later deleted a harbor-entry line to protect her sister Ida's unrelated fuel-smuggling run. Her tampering obscures Freja's skiff but was not part of the murder.

## The older crime: the *Eidolon*

In February 1995, the ferry *Eidolon* sank during a storm, killing twenty-three people. The official finding blamed an exceptional cross-current. The real chain was:

1. Nils, then night dispatcher, accepted payment to carry undeclared solvent drums.
2. Oskar disabled Saint Vigga's radio beacon so the unscheduled route would not be logged.
3. Annika, then a junior hydrographer, altered a current table after Nils threatened her father's livelihood.
4. When drums broke loose, junior deck officer Freja opened the vehicle-deck sea door to jettison them. She then chained the passenger stair to keep witnesses away. Floodwater entered through the open door; seven passengers died behind the chained stair.
5. Emil falsified toxicology and time-of-death notes to conceal solvent exposure and the delayed rescue.

Annika secretly removed the bridge recorder during the recovery operation. For nine years she used it to extract money and professional favors from Nils while allowing the false official account to stand. Recently she learned that journalist Petra Dahl had triggered a new inquiry. Annika planned to sell Petra a selectively edited copy that implicated Nils, Freja, Emil, and Oskar while hiding her altered current table. This is why every conspirator fears the recorder, while several innocent people distrust the victim.

The recorder was never in the brass chart cylinder Nils seized. Annika hid it inside the counterweight housing of the marine laboratory's nineteenth-century tide clock. Her private index at Saint Oda's archive and a weight discrepancy at the lab jointly reveal the hiding place. The intact recorder proves every role in the 1995 crime and records Nils planning the present abduction during a careless rehearsal call accidentally captured when Annika tested it.

## The intended apparent solution

Around the midpoint, evidence can coherently implicate Karin Ravn:

- She threatened Annika publicly.
- Blue museum glass is in Annika's cuff.
- A museum conservation tank contains diluted sedative residue from Karin's unrelated restoration fraud.
- Karin possessed a Saint Vigga exhibition key.
- She falsified acquisition records and expected Annika to expose her.
- A witness sees her loading a wrapped, human-sized sculpture into the museum van.

The theory is wrong. The sculpture delivery is timestamped at 18:58 on the mainland-facing quay, making Karin unable to drown Annika at Tidegate or stage Saint Vigga. Mineral comparison shows Annika's lung water came from the Tidegate rainwater cistern, not the museum tank. The blue cullet was planted after death; it is embedded in wet wool but absent from the underlying skin and wound margins.

## Proof required for a complete accusation

The players must establish all four chains:

1. **True scene and cause** — sedation, deliberate freshwater drowning at Tidegate, and postmortem staging at Saint Vigga.
2. **Abduction and movement** — Gull House service corridor, maintenance van, Tidegate card, skiff route, submerged fort landing, and bell lift.
3. **Role assignment** — Nils planned; Emil drugged; Freja killed and transported; Oskar staged and locked the scene.
4. **Motive and twist** — the *Eidolon* recorder, Annika's blackmail and complicity, and the reason the conspirators' accounts overlap without any one person being able to complete the entire timeline.

Naming only Freja is an incomplete solution. Naming only Nils is an incomplete solution. Karin is innocent of murder but guilty of acquisition fraud. Solveig is guilty of evidence tampering. Leif Arden sold restricted seabed data. These crimes remain real and explain their lies.
