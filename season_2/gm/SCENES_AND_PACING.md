# Season 2 scenes and pacing — *The Ninth Bell*

> GAME-MASTER EYES ONLY. Locations are clue environments, not vending machines. Arrival never grants every listed discovery.

## Structure

The case is designed as a flexible four-act clue graph across twelve locations. Eight or more locations should normally be required. Do not force an order, but keep decisive comparisons cross-location.

### Act I — the dry drowning

Opening locations: Signal House, Saint Vigga Sea Fort, Marine Laboratory, Glassworks Museum.

Goals:

- Establish freshwater drowning in a dry saltwater setting without naming Tidegate.
- Present Karin, Leif, Solveig, Nils, and Freja as credible suspects.
- Establish the blue-glass lead and missing brass cylinder.
- Let players learn that Annika accumulated enemies.

Do not reveal the *Eidolon* conspiracy yet. References should initially sound like grief, disputed compensation, or island folklore.

### Act II — the convincing wrong answer

Likely locations: Glassworks Museum, Gull House, Saltline Clinic, Saint Oda Archive, Ferry Terminal.

The Karin theory should become plausible through `r1`–`r5`. Allow players to confront her. She can confess acquisition fraud under sufficient pressure, but she truthfully denies murder. Her verified 18:58 sculpture delivery and the absence of tissue reaction around the glass are the first major cracks.

Keep Nils suspicious but not unique. His hotel meeting can be explained as blackmail negotiation; the early van evidence identifies a fleet class shared by six vehicles.

### Act III — the impossible lone timeline

Likely locations: Tidegate, Salvage Yard, Weather Station, return visits.

The water comparison fixes Tidegate. The skiff route and bell lift prove movement after death. At this point Freja may look like the lone killer. Make that theory coherent but incomplete: verified sightings place Freja on the water while someone separately stops the fort clock, alters clinic records, and appears at the ferry board meeting.

The central twist is earned when players realize the evidence describes handoffs. Do not announce “conspiracy.” Let the time-and-role conflict force the conclusion.

### Act IV — roles and the ninth recording

Likely locations: Rask House, Saint Oda Archive, Marine Laboratory return.

Annika's index plus the tide-clock discrepancy reveals the recorder. It recontextualizes the victim as both coerced participant and long-term blackmailer. The rehearsal call assigns present-night planning roles, but the fatal act still requires Tidegate physical evidence against Freja.

The finale is an accusation, pursuit, or controlled confrontation depending on whom the investigators warned and protected.

## Location scenes

### Old Signal House — headquarters and temporary mortuary

Opening sensory frame: wind flexes the telegraph wires; Annika lies beneath canvas on a chart table; fresh water drips from hair that smells faintly of iron rather than sea salt.

Opportunities:

- Autopsy: `a1_lung_water_profile`, `a2_pressure_bruises`, `a3_midazolam_batch`.
- Solveig's access behavior: early category-level suspicion, later `x2_solveig_deleted_log`.
- Emil can explain away one anomaly, not all three.

### Saint Vigga Sea Fort

Opening: a granite battery reached by a narrow causeway at low tide; the bell chamber is dry, iron-cold, and apparently sealed.

Opportunities:

- `r1_blue_cullet`, public-gallery key mismatch, stopped clock.
- After submerged landing is known: `b7_bell_lift_use`.
- After Oskar tool suspicion: `c4_oskar_clock_tool`.

Danger: the submerged landing floods quickly. Warn with tide marks, audible surge, and Erik Sund's forecast before trapping anyone.

### Vesperholm Marine Laboratory

Opportunities:

- Annika's work, missing decoy cylinder, Leif's data sale.
- Water comparisons `a1`/`a4`.
- `d3_altered_current_table`, `d5_recorder_hiding_place`, `d6_original_recorder`.

The tide clock is visible from the first visit but should not be searchable as a magic container. Its weight discrepancy must be earned.

### Vesper Glassworks Museum

Opportunities:

- `r2_karin_threat`, `r3_museum_tank_residue`, `r4_fort_exhibition_key`, `r5_wrapped_figure`.
- `a5_postmortem_cullet`, `d4_chained_stair`, `x1_karin_forgery`.

This is the strongest false-solution hub. Karin's crimes are real; exoneration for murder must not erase them.

### Gull House Hotel

Marta begins hospitable and evasive, not interview-ready. Respect earns access; aggression makes a warrant or alternate entry necessary.

Opportunities:

### Saltline Clinic

### Tidegate Pumping Station

Opportunities:

Danger: Nils may remotely cycle a gate after the investigators publicly identify Tidegate. Telegraph the machinery start and provide ladder, manual brake, and exterior rescue route.

### Moller Salvage Yard

Opportunities:

### Saint Oda Chapel and Archive

Opportunities:

Pastor Elias protects privacy but is not obstructive when shown a concrete risk to Petra.

### North Light Weather Station

Opportunities:

- Tide and wind reconstruction for `b6_skiff_route`.
- Erik Sund's discovery timeline and Oskar's false instrument-check claim.

### Rask House and Ferry Office

Opportunities:

- `c1_nils_planning_payment`, burner-phone packaging, empty brass cylinder.
- Anneli's partial alibi and Kasper's fort-key handoff.

Do not permit a simple office search to produce a confession. The financial proof requires fragments from the hotel, clinic, and Oskar.

## NPC danger schedule

Danger is progress-triggered, not chat-count-triggered.

- After `a4_tidegate_match`, Petra receives a blank call and reports someone outside the chapel.
- After any two role clues, Jesper finds his brake line cut and asks for protection.
- After `d2_annika_payment_index`, Ida sees a flare from the old quarry and receives a message using Solveig's name.
- Only after the relevant warning is delivered and ignored may an attack occur.
- No protected NPC dies automatically. Concrete protection plans work.

## Ending variants

- **Full reconstruction:** all four roles proven; recorder preserved; innocent helpers protected.
- **Fatal act only:** Freja proven but Nils's planning or Oskar/Emil roles remain contestable; conspiracy partially escapes.
- **Mastermind only:** Nils exposed but Freja's deliberate act unproven; defense frames death as accident.
- **Wrong Karin accusation:** her fraud is exposed, but a fresh Tidegate fact provides one fair recovery route.
- **Recorder destroyed:** circumstantial solution remains possible if the distributed role chains are complete.
