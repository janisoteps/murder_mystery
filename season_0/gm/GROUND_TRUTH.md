# Season 0 ground truth

> STOP: GAME-MASTER SPOILERS. Players should not read this file before completing the rehearsal.

This document is immutable once the rehearsal begins.

## Crime

Reverend Abel Croft killed Cal Mercer at Harker Grain Elevator at approximately 6:12 p.m. Croft had been diverting donations from the chapel restoration fund to pay a private institution that houses his violently ill adult son. Cal discovered the altered ledgers while repairing wiring shared by the chapel office and threatened to expose him unless Croft paid him.

Croft arranged a meeting at the elevator. During an argument, Cal recorded part of their exchange on a pocket microcassette recorder. Croft struck Cal behind the ear with the chapel's red-painted iron processional staff, bound his wrists with a cut length of chapel bell rope, and pushed him into the empty grain dryer while he was still alive. Croft engaged the circulation fan to make the death resemble an industrial accident. Cal died from a combination of blood loss, aspiration of grain dust, and trauma from the rotating paddles.

Croft took Cal's wallet and telephone but did not notice the recorder beneath the dryer housing. He returned the staff to the chapel after wiping it with altar wine. A chip of red enamel remained embedded in Cal's scalp.

## Fixed timeline

- 5:35 p.m. — Cal leaves the North Star Motor Court with his recorder and photographs of Croft's altered ledger.
- 5:52 p.m. — Croft closes the chapel office and cuts a short section from the bell rope.
- 6:02 p.m. — Ruth Harker sees Croft's dark station wagon turn down Elevator Road.
- 6:07 p.m. — Cal begins recording at the grain elevator.
- 6:12 p.m. — Croft strikes Cal and forces him into the dryer.
- 6:19 p.m. — Croft stops at Ruth's Diner to wash blood from his cuff. He claims he spilled altar wine.
- 6:31 p.m. — Croft returns the weapon to the chapel.
- 6:48 p.m. — Deputy Warren Sloane discovers the body after an automated machinery alarm.
- 7:20 p.m. — Mara Voss and Elias Reed receive the briefing at the Sheriff's Annex.

## NPC truths

### Reverend Abel Croft

The murderer. Controlled in public, terrified beneath the surface. He initially admits meeting Cal but places it at the chapel two days earlier. If confronted with two physical clues, he claims Cal attacked him. If the recorder is found or three independent clue chains converge, he attempts to lure an isolated investigator into the chapel bell tower and cut the ladder loose.

### Deputy Warren Sloane

Not the murderer. He hated Cal because Cal was having an affair with Sloane's estranged wife. Sloane lied about recognizing Cal's motel key because he had followed him there earlier in the week. His hostility and lie make him the principal red herring.

### Ruth Harker

Saw Croft's station wagon and saw him wash a dark stain from his right cuff. She initially withholds the second fact because Croft helped her daughter through a crisis. Firm but empathetic questioning reveals it.

### Iris Bell

Rents room 6 to Cal under the name Carl Moore. She found the ledger photographs while snooping and kept one as leverage. She lies about entering the room.

## Ending standard

The investigators can make a defensible accusation once they establish motive, physical linkage, and opportunity. The strongest proof is the recorder. An accusation based only on Croft's station wagon or financial motive is insufficient.
