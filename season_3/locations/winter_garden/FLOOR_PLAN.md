## Scale and structure

- Overall enclosed footprint: approximately 60 × 32 metres.
- Main Palm Hall: approximately 35 × 23 metres, 12 metres high beneath the central glass ridge.
- Three connected architectural zones: public conservatory, west service wing, and east entrance/hotel connection.
- Upper gallery wraps three sides of the Palm Hall and is reached by two stairs plus a compact service lift.
- A sheltered exterior approach and service yard make exterior-to-interior movement visible rather than instantaneous.

## Ground-floor spaces

- Sheltered exterior approach and public entrance vestibule.
- Coat alcove, reception point, and east hotel connector.
- Palm Hall with intersecting tiled paths, planted beds, two reflecting basins, citrus apse, and seating bays.
- West stair, east stair, and service lift.
- Potting room, delivery vestibule, wash room, tool store, and service yard.
- Wide circulation loops around the planting beds; no dead-end decorative aisles.

## Upper-level spaces

- North, west, and east gallery walks overlooking the Palm Hall.
- Two viewing bays and a north gallery bridge.
- Gallery restoration bay connected to the west stair.
- East landing connected to the hotel side and service lift.
- Open central void preserves the conservatory's scale and long sightlines.
