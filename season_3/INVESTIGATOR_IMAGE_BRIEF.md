# Season 3 investigator image brief — Mara Keller

Player-safe visual brief for externally generated reference artwork. Contains no mystery solution, evidence, or hidden character information.

## Character identity

**Mara Keller**, 40, a Central European major-crimes investigator experienced in scene reconstruction and long-form witness interviews. She is the sole player-controlled investigator in *The Winter Garden*.

Mara should look like a real working professional in November 1998: capable, physically grounded, quietly distinctive, and comfortable spending a long night moving between snow, hotel corridors, workshops, and humid thermal interiors. Her appearance must not prescribe the player's personality, dialogue, emotional response, or investigative choices.

## Master image prompt

```text
Create a highly detailed full-body character reference image for the sole playable investigator in a prestige isometric 3D murder-mystery game set in a fictional Alpine spa town in late November 1998.

CHARACTER
Mara Keller, a white Central European woman aged 40. She has a believable, lived-in face rather than a fashion-model face: softly angular oval facial structure, moderately high cheekbones, a firm rounded jaw, straight medium-length nose with a subtle natural irregularity along the bridge, defined but not sculpted lips, faint smile lines, and light under-eye texture from an adult working life. Her skin is fair with neutral-to-cool undertones, natural pores, slight winter redness across the cheeks and nose, a few small sun freckles, and no glamour retouching.

Her eyes are deep gray-brown with a faint amber ring close to the pupils. They should feel attentive and steady without making her stare intensely at the viewer. Her eyebrows are medium brown, mostly straight with a gentle natural arch, individually visible hairs, and no heavily shaped makeup. Her expression is neutral, alert, and approachable: relaxed mouth, focused eyes, no smile for the camera, no scowl, no suspicion-signaling pose.

Her hair is dark chestnut brown with a few subtle naturally lighter strands, thick but practical, cut to just above the shoulders. It is parted slightly left, tucked behind the right ear, with a loose imperfect wave and several fine strands displaced by cold air. The cut should be recognizably late-1990s and understated, with no contemporary balayage, severe bob, elaborate styling, or glossy salon finish.

Mara is approximately 172 cm tall, with realistic adult proportions and a capable medium build. She is neither extremely slender nor bodybuilder-muscular. Give her naturally broad enough shoulders for the coat, strong legs from ordinary walking, relaxed hands, anatomically accurate joints, and a stable balanced stance. Preserve a clear, recognizable silhouette at isometric gameplay distance.

CLOTHING
Her main outer layer is a charcoal-blue knee-length wool field coat designed for real cold weather. It has a gently shaped but practical cut, structured shoulders softened by wear, broad notched lapels, dark horn buttons, two deep flap pockets, a center back vent, and subtle seam construction that will translate well to a detailed 3D garment. The wool should have visible short fibers, slight creasing at elbows and waist, softened pocket edges, and a little dampness or fine melted snow at the shoulders and hem. The coat is worn open enough to reveal the layers beneath.

Under the coat she wears a muted warm-gray lambswool crew-neck sweater with fine ribbing at the collar, cuffs, and hem. Beneath it is an off-white cotton poplin shirt: small period-appropriate collar, only a narrow section visible at the neck and cuffs, never bright optical white. Her trousers are high-waisted, straight-cut dark brown-gray wool trousers with a pressed crease that has softened from wear. They fit naturally through the hip and knee and fall cleanly over the boots; no skinny fit, leggings, cargo pockets, or modern stretch fabric.

Her footwear is a pair of dark oxblood-brown leather lace-up ankle boots with low practical heels, rounded almond toes, welted soles, faint salt marks, realistic creasing across the vamp, and light wear at the edges. They should look stable on wet tile and snow rather than fashionable or tactical.

A narrow desaturated burgundy wool scarf sits once around her neck and hangs asymmetrically inside the coat, creating a restrained warm-color accent. It is soft, lightly pilled, and functional. Do not make it long, dramatic, windblown, or bright red.

ACCESSORIES AND INVESTIGATIVE KIT
Across her body is a compact dark-brown vegetable-tanned leather satchel on a wide adjustable strap, resting at her left hip. It should be large enough for a notebook, compact camera, gloves, and evidence envelopes, but not resemble a modern laptop bag. The leather has a matte finish, fine grain, gently darkened corners, brass roller buckle, simple flap closure, and believable weight pulling on the strap. Keep the satchel design clean enough to rig around the body later.

In her left hand she holds a small closed black clothbound investigator's notebook, thumb resting along its spine. Her right hand hangs naturally and remains fully visible for anatomy and later modeling. A slim late-1990s silver analog wristwatch with a dark leather band is visible at the left cuff. She wears no visible badge, gun, holster, radio headset, body camera, smartphone, smartwatch, tactical vest, or modern equipment.

Add very small understated details: plain silver stud earrings, a simple key ring partly visible inside one coat pocket, and thin dark leather gloves tucked into the satchel's outer sleeve. No wedding ring should be emphasized and no accessory should imply a fixed private backstory.

POSE AND CAMERA
Show the entire character from head to boots, with nothing cropped. Use a relaxed standing three-quarter pose, body turned about 20 degrees away from the camera while the face turns gently back toward it. Her feet are about shoulder-width apart, with weight distributed naturally and one foot half a step forward. Shoulders are relaxed. Keep both hands visible and anatomically clear. Avoid crossed arms, hands in pockets, crouching, walking, action posing, contrapposto fashion posing, weapon poses, dramatic coat movement, or exaggerated detective gestures.

Camera at approximately chest height using an 85 mm full-frame-equivalent portrait lens, far enough away to include the full body without wide-angle distortion. Vertical 2:3 composition. The face, hands, outerwear construction, satchel, trousers, and boots must all be sharply resolved. Preserve realistic head-to-body proportion; do not enlarge the head into a stylized game-character proportion.

LIGHTING AND BACKGROUND
Place her against a simple warm-gray studio background with a very subtle cool-to-warm gradient and a matte floor plane. Use soft neutral key light from front-left, a restrained cool rim light from rear-right, and gentle fill that preserves facial planes and garment texture. The lighting should resemble a premium film costume and character reference shoot, not a dramatic story scene. A soft contact shadow grounds both boots. Color management should be natural and restrained, with accurate skin and textile colors.

STYLE AND QUALITY
Photorealistic cinematic character-design photography, extremely high material fidelity, anatomically accurate adult human, natural skin texture, individually resolved hair strands, convincing layered clothing, physically believable wool, cotton, leather, brass, and dampness. Prestige European crime drama, quiet visual intelligence, late-1990s authenticity, subtle humanity. The result must serve as a reliable identity and costume reference for creation of an ultra-detailed realistic 3D character.

Do not place her at the hotel, winter garden, crime scene, or any identifiable story location. Do not include another person, body, blood, clue, evidence board, document text, police tape, weapon, vehicle, architecture, decorative plant, snowstorm, or narrative action.

NO TEXT anywhere in the image: no title, name, caption, labels, logos, watermark, signature, UI, or typography.
```

## Recommended generation setup

- Generate vertically at the service's highest practical resolution, ideally at least 2048 × 3072.
- Use photorealistic or cinematic-photography mode rather than illustration or stylized-concept mode.
- Keep prompt adherence and detail high, with stylization low to moderate.
- Generate several variations, but do not change age, facial structure, hair, body type, coat, scarf color, boots, or satchel between them.
- Prioritize a coherent face, natural hands, correct full-body proportions, clear layered clothing, and an uncropped silhouette over dramatic atmosphere.
- Do not ask the generator for a multi-panel turnaround in this first pass. Image models often make the identity and clothing inconsistent between panels.

## Selection criteria

Choose the result in which:

1. Mara looks like a believable 40-year-old professional rather than a model wearing a detective costume.
2. Her face feels distinctive at close range but remains neutral enough for the player to inhabit.
3. The full outfit is readable from head to boots, with sensible layering and period-correct shapes.
4. Both hands are correct and visible.
5. The coat, scarf, satchel, trousers, and boots form a strong silhouette at reduced size.
6. No modern object, text, badge, weapon, story clue, or setting detail has slipped into the image.

Once a final identity image is selected, use it as an image reference for separate front, side, back, face, hands, and expression sheets. Do not generate those until the identity has been approved.
