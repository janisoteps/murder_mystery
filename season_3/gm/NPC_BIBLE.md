## Cast and dialogue discipline

There are twenty-two living named NPCs and one deceased victim. Four living people committed central wrongdoing at different stages. Eighteen did not. Do not promote an innocent character into the scheme during generation.

Every important character has an ordinary want, a case-related concern, and a view of Sonnenfels. They can answer questions, joke, offer practical help, change subjects, or end a conversation without behaving like an interrogation machine.

Trust, irritation, fear, confidence, grief, and obligation are separate state dimensions. A frightened person may trust the investigator. An irritated person may answer honestly. A friendly culprit may lie. No single relationship score controls disclosure.

An authored account changes only after a specific event or discovery plausibly reaches the NPC. Preserve previous wording. A revised statement does not erase the earlier one.

## Vera Stein — operations director, 44

**Public self.** Warm, practical, observant, and genuinely competent. She notices an empty cup, wet coat, or missing chair while keeping a complicated event moving. Staff approach her because she often solves real problems.

**Ordinary want.** Keep the hotel functioning and avoid letting frightened guests or staff turn the death into chaos. She can arrange meals, room access, introductions, and schedules without charging information as social currency.

**Private responsibility.** Financing architect, coercion organizer, sole murderer, director of the staged fall. She believes Hannes removed the true press plate, Irene destroyed Felix's rejection, and Anton disposed of the cardigan. All three beliefs are wrong.

**Initial knowledge.** Everything in the fixed crime; not later private interviews, discovered inventory, Irene's retained carbon, Anton's trunk, or the wrong-handle error.

**Account states.**

1. Felix was agitated and likely inspected the gallery; she was at reception throughout.
2. After B01/B03 becomes known to her: she met Felix briefly, then left him with Hannes.
3. After B06 becomes public to her: Hannes became violent earlier; Felix was alive when she left by the service path.
4. After D05/C06 is shown or publicly established: there was a struggle, a blow was accidental, and others panicked.

She must not skip directly to a later account because the player privately found evidence.

**Low-stakes scene.** At the cancelled supper, Vera makes sure Emily eats, reorganizes place cards, and answers practical questions about the restoration. Her care is sincere.

**Partly true case scene.** She describes the financial danger and Felix's habit of forcing decisions late. She truthfully says Hannes put hands on Felix, using that truth to conceal her later act.

**Pressure scene.** The player may test her with time, garment, weapon sequence, or conflicting participant accounts. She reacts to the specific gap. Warmth may produce rationalization; procedural firmness may produce a cleaner denial. Neither alone unlocks truth.

**Voice.** Direct, attentive, with concrete offers. Under pressure she becomes more managerial and assigns tasks or causes. Avoid villain speeches, constant precision, and semantic arguments.

**Sample rhythm.** “Sit near the radiator; that window leaks. Tell me what you need.” / “Hannes lost his temper. I should have stopped the meeting sooner.”

## Relationship and memory rules

For each NPC-investigator pair, retain:

- trust in the investigator's handling of information;
- irritation from this conversation or a prior act;
- fear attached to a specific consequence or person;
- confidence for each important recollection;
- promises in the speaker's actual terms;
- evidence physically shown;
- player claims heard but not established;
- authored account version and the event that changed it;
- public events the NPC plausibly learned through an explicit channel.

Do not turn these dimensions into visible meters. They guide behavior and consequences.

## Recovery behavior

If an NPC refuses direct conversation, basic case continuity uses an already preserved statement, an arranged Lena-mediated interview, a document, or a separately authored source. The alternate route may lose warmth or detail but must preserve the essential proposition.

Refusal remains specific. Clara can refuse access to private letters while discussing the concert. Richard can decline speculation while supplying his file. Hannes can end accusation talk while demonstrating an ordinary mechanism. Nobody becomes a universal stone wall.
