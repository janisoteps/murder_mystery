# Season 3 scenes and pacing — *The Winter Garden*

> GAME-MASTER EYES ONLY. Never expose hidden movements, act labels, missed-source recovery, or intended theories. `GROUND_TRUTH.md` governs the fixed event. This file governs investigation rhythm and world change.

## Duration and structure

Target approximately twenty hours for a first player who explores carefully, speaks with a broad portion of the cast, tests competing explanations, and follows human threads. A direct evidential route can finish earlier. Never slow a correct supported player to meet the target.

The season has four investigation movements. They are changes in the player's likely question, not mandatory acts or invisible locks.

| Movement | Likely first-play range | Driving question | Recontextualization |
|---|---:|---|---|
| I. The broken gallery | 0–4 hours | Was this a fall, negligence, or an assault? | The palm-hall scene was arranged; Felix was fatally injured in the potting room |
| II. The man at the service door | 3–9 hours | Did Hannes kill Felix during a coercive confrontation? | Hannes committed real violence and concealed an object, but the fatal assault occurred after he left |
| III. Four different concealments | 8–15 hours | Which lies protect the murder, and which protect other wrongdoing? | Irene, Hannes, Anton, and Vera do not share the same knowledge or account |
| IV. The fourteen rooms | 13–20 hours | Who killed Felix, who assisted at each stage, and why? | The physical sequence and consent fraud divide the roles and support the final confrontation |

Ranges overlap. Players may understand Schedule East-14 before resolving Hannes, or find the wrong weapon comparison before meeting Clara. Progress follows discoveries and explicit public events rather than movement numbers.

## Activity budget

The target assumes approximately six hours of close 3D exploration, four and a half of dialogue, three of physical manipulation and spatial reconstruction, three of document/timeline comparison, two of revisits and world events, and one and a half of accusation and aftermath. These activities overlap. Repeated travel, waiting, failed clicks, unskippable animations, and NPC repetition do not count as intended play.

Major case entries should arrive at an average pace of one every twenty to thirty minutes during focused investigation, often as an update to an existing entry. Quiet sequences and clusters are both desirable. Avoid releasing six dense text records at once.

## Location tiers

### Flagship 3D environments

Winter Garden, Grand Hotel, Restoration Workshop, Thermal Baths, East Residence, and Panorama Pavilion. Each needs a distinctive interaction grammar, two or more meaningful states, rich ordinary life, and enough scale for controlled zoom.

### Focused 3D environments

Architect's Lodging, Funicular Station, and Gardener's Cottage/Nursery. These are detailed but more compact, with one dominant system and one return purpose.

### Compact social/support environments

Village Café, Municipal Reading Room, and Police/Medical Room. They remain explorable and visually specific without receiving the same geometry budget as the flagship spaces.

This tiering protects quality. It is not visible as a ranking on the map.

## Movement I — The broken gallery

### Opening handover

At 20:05, Lena meets the investigator at the Winter Garden entrance. She states only public facts: Felix was found beneath the incomplete gallery, the area has been secured, Miriam could not confirm a simple fall, and several people entered during discovery. Lena asks where the investigator wants to begin.

The opening teaches movement, adjustable isometric zoom, object focus, conversation, and case-entry presentation in context. One neutral object can demonstrate the large modal before the first important exhibit; collecting evidence should not feel like a tutorial interrupting a corpse.

### Available questions

- Does body position and the planter support a fall?
- What was actually unfinished on the gallery?
- Why did the coat and cordon change?
- Where did the plant trolley come from?
- Who was nearby before the alarm?

The potting room is visible and searchable from the opening. A03 and A04 may establish the true scene early. The initial medical record should distinguish “not well explained by a single fall” from the later firm differentiation of injuries.

### Human scenes

Emily can describe finding Felix while shaken and still care about the returned plant. Jakob moves between preserving plants and helping Lena. Nora is at the Workshop once unlocked. Vera offers practical support and behaves as a credible operations director. Anton handles distressed guests. These behaviors must exist independently of guilt-signaling animations.

### Movement turn

Once the player has enough of A01/A03/A04/A05, the status language changes from “possible fall” to “body likely moved from another part of the Winter Garden.” Do not announce murder location if the player has not established it. Unlock suggested comparisons rather than highlighting a suspect.

### Missed-source protection

Lena's initial photographs preserve positions. Miriam's notes preserve discovery handling. Scene transfer never erases direct trace records. Celia's bounded statement can be taken later at the Pavilion or through Lena.

## Movement II — The man at the service door

### Investigation texture

The Workshop, Hotel, and Winter Garden service route carry this movement. It should feel physical: screen placement, door control, actual restraint trace, changing job record, contractor hamper, and witnessed work interval.

### Strong Hannes theory

Allow the player to build this fully:

Hannes stood to lose his firm, arranged the cleared approach, restrained Felix, had victim blood on his apron, hid a bloody iron press component, changed the time record, blamed Nora, and planned to leave in the morning. Vera may encourage this theory by admitting an argument happened but claiming Hannes remained after she left.

Do not weaken the theory through ominous narration or make Hannes implausibly hateful. His practical helpfulness should make the evidence more unsettling.

### Fair breaks in the theory

The breaks are available through distinct work:

1. A05 separates the split lip/wrist injuries from fatal head impacts.
2. B06 gives Hannes an overlapping workshop interval for the fatal assault.
3. C03/C05 shows that the bloody handle he hid was not the fatal object.
4. A06 places the fatal assault before the trolley route within the interval Vera remained.

The player need not find all four before questioning the theory. Two strong strands plus a coherent account can move the investigation onward.

### World event: scheduled supplier trip

Hannes has a real 08:40 supplier trip the next morning. The Workshop shows packed parts and a goods docket from the first visit; this is not spawned when he becomes suspicious. If the player requests an interview in time, Lena can ask him to remain. If he leaves, he is reachable later through a scheduled telephone interview and his workshop records remain. The change affects immediacy and atmosphere, never solvability.

### Movement turn

The discovery that Hannes removed the wrong object should be active. It justifies reopening the botanical press and changes the question from “why hide the weapon?” to “what did Vera make him believe he was hiding?” Do not immediately reveal the true plate or Vera's role.

## Movement III — Four different concealments

### Investigation texture

This movement joins the Hotel, Residence, Pavilion, Baths, and records routes. The player classifies sources rather than merely collecting more of them.

### Irene

Her forged approval, removed papers, and changed meeting note are independently provable. She believes Hannes caused the death. Once East-14 becomes public to the residents, she sees that Vera is still trying to enforce the schedule and requests a bounded conversation. She may disclose the retained carbon because its continued concealment now harms people she knows. If the player declines, Lena can recover it with an established document rationale.

### Anton

His call, interrupted luggage work, trolley contact, and Celia's sighting establish after-death movement. A focused interview can reveal his mistaken initial belief and later observation of the second injury. He may refuse to name Vera. The garment remains recoverable after the trunk's relevance is established.

### Vera

Her account changes only when she plausibly learns that a prior version has failed:

- Public opening: at reception throughout; Felix likely inspected the gallery.
- Private meeting established: she met Felix briefly and left him with Hannes.
- Hannes workshop interval established: Hannes lost control earlier; Felix was alive when she left by another route.
- Cardigan/weapon sequence established: a struggle occurred, the blow was accidental, and others panicked.

Each version is preserved. She cannot sense hidden inventory or account for evidence not shown or made public.

### Neutral corrections

Amalia's time correction, Nora's cordon correction, Benno's clarification of “around all evening,” and Jakob's unlogged plant move teach the player that a changed statement is not automatically a guilty lie. These corrections should occur in different tones and contexts.

### Movement turn

The investigator recognizes that the people concealing events disagree about the death because they received different explanations. Irene's Hannes belief, Hannes's accident belief, Anton's Vera suspicion, and Vera's shifting blame cannot all describe the same knowledge. This is a role puzzle grounded in people, not a UI minigame requiring exact labels.

## Movement IV — The fourteen rooms

### Investigation texture

The Residence turns finance into space. The Reading Room supplies the clean comparison. Felix's Lodging gives his intended action and unresolved responsibility. The Winter Garden returns as the physical proof site. The Pavilion provides personal context and photographs without becoming a mandatory suspect branch.

### Document discipline

Only four document ideas matter to the core:

1. East-14 changes fourteen occupied rooms and a shared winter room.
2. The approved agreement preserves them.
3. Felix's signature and staff consent were falsified.
4. Felix planned to suspend certification at 19:30.

All other records corroborate a person, time, or work order. Do not make the player read repetitive financing pages.

### The true weapon and garment

C05 provides cause to examine the compression plates. The large evidence view shows the inverted felt seat and retained trace without labeling it “the murder weapon.” C06 becomes a comparison after professional support.

D04 begins as a material class. D05 becomes specific only after justified recovery and side-by-side edge/material work. Saskia's image proves Vera wore the garment earlier; blood-wicking and potting-room location distinguish it from later public contact.

### Residence meeting

Once East-14 is explicitly disclosed to at least one resident representative, Rosa and Pavel call a meeting for the next available morning/afternoon band. The player may attend or receive agreed minutes. Richard argues the real financing risk. Residents understand the terms and decide what they want recorded.

This event causes Irene's approach and gives Vera a reason to keep defending the schedule. It is not a morality test and does not expose the murderer.

### Movement turn

The player can now divide restraint, fatal assault, movement, disposal, paperwork, and motive. F06 supports a free-form role reconstruction. A correct early synthesis may have reached this point with fewer personal branches.

## Final reconstruction

The player can request a formal case presentation at any time. Lena reviews only whether the requested confrontation has grounded support and identifies one unresolved link if necessary without naming the answer.

The final scene takes place in the East Residence's shared winter room before the plan meeting. The investigator chooses whether resident representatives remain and which account to test first. Vera, Hannes, Irene, and Anton have conflicting incentives; their dialogue depends on previously established accounts and evidence shown.

The player need not recite every clue. The evaluator checks the six propositions in `CLUE_MATRIX.md`. If one accomplice role is vague, Lena or that participant asks a focused question. Do not turn an almost-complete correct reconstruction into a generic failure.

Vera's response is not required to be a confession. Official action follows the supported reconstruction. The player retains control of their tone and conclusion.

## Epilogue states

The core epilogue always records the exposed death, preservation of evidence, and suspension of the false accident account.

Variable human outcomes draw only from actual play:

- whether Clara's private material remained private and what she chose to preserve;
- whether Nora's genuine safety lapse was separated from homicide blame;
- whether Amalia's corrected statement was treated accurately;
- whether the residents received the relevant documents before negotiations;
- whether Irene, Hannes, or Anton gave useful voluntary accounts;
- what promises the investigator made and kept.

None changes the murderer, proof, or basic access to completion.

## Major location states and interaction grammar

### Winter Garden — spatial reconstruction

**State 1:** secured night scene, active condensation and mist systems, limited staff movement. Questions concern fall, true scene, and route.

**State 2:** daylight controlled access after first preservation. Previously seen traces remain documented; physical systems can be demonstrated. The wrong-handle result makes the press newly relevant through reasoning, not a spawned hotspot.

**State 3:** released public zones, gardening work resumes, final optional quiet scene. The player can understand the building as a lived place after knowing how it was misused.

Interactions: zoomed sightline study, screen movement demonstration, trolley wheel rotation, press examination, plan-chest focus, safe mist-system demonstration, ordinary plant care.

### Grand Hotel — social choreography

**State 1:** cancelled reception and staff response. Public rooms, service corridors, porter desk, bar, and offices contain overlapping people.

**State 2:** breakfast and account pressure. NPC locations change; Irene and Anton face different decisions; Vera remains operationally useful.

**State 3:** aftermath and staff consequences. Essential records remain accessible through custody.

Interactions: trace luggage/service routes, review switchboard fields, compare cloakroom identities, use office reading light, observe sightline gaps, ordinary food/service actions.

### Restoration Workshop — construction sequence

**State 1:** night work and real gallery questions. Nora demonstrates railing; Hannes appears practically competent.

**State 2:** morning supplier preparation and false-record comparison. Packed goods exist before the player acts.

**State 3:** Hannes absent/present/official-call variant; Nora's crew corrects the rail after release.

Interactions: assemble a neutral sample joint, inspect job layers, weigh components, trace tool storage, operate a safe mechanism, compare actual work with drawings.

### Thermal Baths — routine and material circulation

**State 1:** limited night operation and linen handling. Apparent sighting and service routes coexist.

**State 2:** daylight bathing routine makes Amalia's sequence physically legible; authorized hamper recovery changes service area.

Interactions: walk check-out route, inspect locker/ticket process, follow linen/hamper handling, examine secured component, observe steam and dry/wet boundaries.

### East Residence — lived consequence and consent

**State 1:** ordinary occupied evening; measurements and access route are experienced in context.

**State 2:** document comparison spreads among residents; shared winter room becomes a meeting place.

**State 3:** final reconstruction and post-case negotiation are separate uses of the same space.

Interactions: walk access detour, overlay proposed plan on current rooms, inspect marks with consent, help with an optional domestic task, attend or review meeting.

### Panorama Pavilion — memory and visual sequence

**State 1:** interrupted rehearsal and preparation prints. Clara's privacy and Celia's sightline remain distinct.

**State 2:** developed contact sheets and corrected exhibition material; an optional later performance offers closure.

Interactions: sequence film frames without false timestamps, reproduce a sightline with camera controls, compare folder identity, adjust neutral exhibition display, hear rehearsal ambience.

## Pacing safety

- Never keep a disproven theory alive through NPC stupidity.
- Never delay a requested report only to meet an act boundary.
- Never force completion of a personal favor for core progress.
- Never replace a missed witness with an omniscient summary; provide their preserved statement or another authored source.
- Never add a second attack or invisible deadline for artificial urgency.
- Never count ordinary conversation as wasted time when it develops a person or place, but allow it to end without a clue.
- Never turn every environmental interaction into text. Physical and ambient response should often be its own reward.
