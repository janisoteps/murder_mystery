# Season 3 clue matrix — *The Winter Garden*

> GAME-MASTER EYES ONLY. `GROUND_TRUTH.md` controls facts. Stable entry IDs are authoring contracts once implementation begins. Never expose undiscovered entries, hidden meanings, proof groups, alternate sources, or final requirements.

## Case-entry model

The season has **36 player-facing case entries**, many of which accumulate sub-observations, images, statements, and later comparisons. Do not split every sub-observation into a separate evidence card. An evolving entry should visibly retain its sources and earlier interpretation when updated.

Entry types:

- **Inventory item:** a portable object explicitly collected after its large image-and-description modal.
- **Secured exhibit:** an object photographed and transferred into Lena's custody; the journal retains its image, observable description, and custody status.
- **Scene record:** photographs and observations from a fixed place.
- **Document:** a relevant page or readable comparison; long records are reduced to the exact player-visible portions that matter.
- **Statement:** what a named person said, including later corrections without overwriting the first wording.
- **Comparison:** an investigator-supported relationship among already discovered sources.

Each entry below states the question it raises, the reasonable actions it suggests, and the later way its meaning can change. Those are authoring aids, not text shown verbatim to the player.

## Proof standards

The evaluator judges propositions and source quality, not raw entry counts. Alternate phrasings and equivalent evidence combinations must work.

### P1 — the gallery fall was staged

Strong route: A01 + A03 + A04 + A05. A02 explains why the cover story looked plausible. D02 or D03 can independently strengthen post-death movement.

An answer based only on “the planter looked strange” is an intuition. Ask what connects the body to another place without confirming the hidden answer.

### P2 — Felix was coerced and Hannes assaulted him earlier

Strong route: B02 + B03 + B05, with B01 or B04 establishing purpose. Hannes's own admission may replace one testimonial link but not all external support.

The player need not use the word “kidnap” or assign a legal category.

### P3 — Hannes did not deliver the fatal blows

Strong route: A05 injury separation + B06 workshop interval + C03/C05 wrong-weapon result. Any two exceptionally strong independent strands may support the conclusion if the player's chronology is coherent.

Do not treat Hannes as innocent generally. The conclusion concerns the fatal blows.

### P4 — Vera killed Felix in the potting room

Strong route: P1 and P3 + C06 + D04/D05 + B06. D01 and D06 strengthen the staging and consciousness-of-guilt chain. E05 supplies motive but cannot replace physical contact.

An unsupported correct name is not enough. A player who lacks the cardigan may still succeed with a materially equivalent combination of exclusive interval, true weapon sequence, Vera's call/control, and a reliable assault-site contact source added during implementation. Do not create that substitute ad hoc during play; author it before release if needed.

### P5 — each accomplice's role

- Irene: E03 + B04 + altered meeting record preserved in F05 or source details.
- Hannes: B02/B05 + C02/C03, while retaining P3.
- Anton: D01/D02/D03, with his statement optional.

The player is rewarded for distinguishing knowledge and timing. A correct principal murderer with vague “everyone helped” language prompts one focused question before completion.

### P6 — motive and intended disclosure

Strong route: E01 + E02/E03 + E04/E05 + E06. The player must understand that consent to occupied-space conversion was falsified and Felix intended to suspend certification. Exact financing arithmetic is irrelevant.

## Anti-shortcut and recovery rules

An early accusation opens a support review. It never changes facial animation, music, marker color, or UI language based on hidden correctness.

Repeated questioning does not yield new facts through persistence. NPCs refer to the current authored account, clarify if a genuine ambiguity exists, or end the subject. External discoveries or believable world events create account changes.

Showing unrelated evidence produces a specific natural response appropriate to the NPC's knowledge. It cannot unlock a registered discovery merely because an attachment was present.

Private-room access requires a concrete investigative basis, consent, or Lena's procedural assistance. Friendship never replaces that basis. Core evidence has preserved official routes if a personal interview ends.

Machine times are labeled by what they measure: call connection, transaction, system cycle, or work entry. Witness memories display confidence and interval. The board never silently equates them.

Future dialogue and client code receive only discovered public forms. Hidden meanings, reveal rules, undiscovered images, and proof evaluation remain server-private.
