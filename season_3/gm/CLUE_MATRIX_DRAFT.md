## Presentation and custody

Types: **item** is a physical possession; **record** is a photograph, document copy, or report; **observation** is something the player directly establishes; **statement** preserves a person's account; **comparison** is a supported relationship between discovered sources. Nonportable objects remain in place with a photograph or observation record.

A physical pickup first opens a large image and an observable description. The explicit collection action adds it to inventory and records source and custody. Objects such as a heavy press weight are secured by Lena after inspection; they should not vanish into a coat pocket. The journal keeps a photograph and custody note.

Descriptions do not announce hidden meaning. “A short strip of dark wool, with an uneven torn edge” is an observation. “The killer's cardigan fragment” is a spoiler and an unsupported conclusion at discovery. A witness account is labeled testimony, not automatically upgraded to fact.

Professional examination is requested through Miriam or Lena. Results have stated scope. Close inspection, photographs, and accessible expert explanations support the same deduction; no clue requires distinguishing nearly identical pixels or knowing real-world forensic jargon.

The alternatives below are source-recovery routes, not permission to conjure an object in a second place. Once an object has been secured, later access uses its preserved photograph, report, or custody record. A carbon copy and its original are related sources, not independent witnesses.

## Proof propositions, not an inventory checklist

The final evaluator should look for supported claims expressed in ordinary language. The following groups are design guidance. They are not secret mandatory combinations, numerical score gates, or a script requiring every discovery.

**P1: the assault occurred in the potting room and the fall scene was arranged.** Strong physical route: A05/A06, A08/A09, A10, and the spatial comparison with A02/A04. Alternative support includes the independently established weapon chain and Anton's account. Merely having a missing railing and a suspicious witness is insufficient.

**P2: the meeting involved organized coercion and selective document seizure.** F05 is the clearest independent account of Felix demanding his bag back; B01/B04/B05/B06 and D10 corroborate the arrangement. Irene's admission can supply details but is unnecessary if the player reconstructs the roles from the independent acts. Initials on one slip alone do not prove all three people's intent.

**P3: Vera committed the fatal assault.** Combine P1 with Vera remaining after Irene and Hannes left, the garment/defensive-contact comparison A11/C05/C09/F04, and the subsequent call and staging chain B02/B12/C07/C08. A detailed truthful admission may strengthen this but cannot replace all external support. Motive and an inconsistent alibi alone are grounds to investigate, not a complete proof.

**P4: Hannes and Irene knowingly assisted before the death and concealed relevant facts afterwards.** Hannes: service closure/coercion plus disposal and changed record. Irene: false approval/selective removal plus changed meeting notes. Their presence at the meeting does not establish that they witnessed the blows. The reconstruction must retain the workshop and office interval evidence when assigning actions.

**P5: Anton assisted after the killing.** B12 and B02 place his call and departure; C07/C08/A12 support movement and rearrangement. C10 is useful but optional. Do not infer killing from carrying a dead person when independent chronology places the carrier elsewhere earlier.

**P6: the planned disclosure threatened a concealed conversion of the residents' rights and staff funds.** D03/D04 establish the material change; D05/D08/D12 show money, work, and human consequence; D09/F06 establish Felix's intended disclosure. Detailed accounting arithmetic is unnecessary once the concrete scheme and participants are supported.

## Information boundaries and alternate reasoning

Unlocking a clue's eligibility does not announce it to the player. Statements are added only when actually spoken or read. Comparisons can be proposed by the player before an authored hint appears; accept any materially supported interpretation, including an explanation not phrased in these headings.

A player may search the potting room immediately, ask for the laundry hamper early, notice a garment discrepancy before reviewing the financing, or request separate witness interviews before anyone suggests them. These are legitimate routes. The story cannot relocate evidence to preserve pacing.

There is no irreplaceable confession, no hidden final recorder, no murder-identifying object available only after an arbitrary hour, and no requirement to solve every personal subplot. If a player collects convincing evidence quickly, the accusation can succeed quickly.

People can refuse to surrender private possessions without cause. Access then follows a concrete investigative reason and Lena's assistance, not a friendliness threshold. Private letters can be confirmed in limited scope. Case-critical access is never predicated on humiliating an innocent person.

If the player names Vera from intuition at the opening, Lena records the theory and asks for its basis. The world does not confirm or deny the hidden role. A supported early accusation gets a genuine evaluation. A vague guess does not trigger confession, seizure of all unrelated possessions, or a victory screen.

First production group: general scene photographs, ordinary work documents, delivery labels, textile close-up, and the botanical press. These support the opening investigation and physical interaction standard.

Second group: garment and weight comparisons, staff and contractor documents, residence sketches, and the contrasting agreement pages. Their real clue versions remain private until integrated into discovered-state delivery.

Third group: personal folder selections, preparation photographs, exhibition materials, and optional community records. These must receive enough artistic care that visual polish does not identify which objects matter to the murder.

Review versions use unrelated text, substitute marks, and neutral file names. Do not ask the player to generate authentic evidence images or read the real agreement discrepancy during asset review. Evidence generation workflow is to be settled later without exposing these contents.
