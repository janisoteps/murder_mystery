# Season 1 visual direction

## Time-of-day variety

Do not depict every location only at dusk or night. Each relevant location should have imagery appropriate to when investigators may visit it.

Final coverage:

- Every location has one exterior and one interior.
- Six exterior views use daylight or bright transitional light for geographic clarity and variety.
- Dusk and night are reserved for locations whose story identity benefits from them.
- Interiors use practical lighting matched to their likely visit time.
- Every investigator and interactive NPC has a portrait with neutral guilt signaling.

The public game manifest presents each exterior/interior pair as a two-image dossier. See `IMAGE_ASSETS.md` for the completed inventory and prompt set.
