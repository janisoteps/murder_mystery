# Season 1 NPC bible

> GAME-MASTER SPOILERS.

All named characters are adults. Background extras have no discoverable clues and do not require portraits.

## Voice and resistance guide

| NPC | Public manner | Knows | Hides | Opens when |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| June Kessler | Warm, exact, quietly intimate | Everything about the murder and 1988 order | Murder, affair, coat, access | Never fully before proof gate; cracks when confronted with three chains |
| Hollis Cade | Blunt, territorial, tired | 1988 cover-up, June's ruthlessness | Altered logs and moved bodies | Documentary contradiction or appeal to protecting current victims |
| Miriam Vale | Clinical, guilt-ridden | True autopsy, false 1988 certificates | Preserved samples | Professional respect and assurance evidence will outlive town politics |
| Naomi Venn | Volatile, incisive, grieving | Celia's broadcast plan, affair rumors | Erased intimate reel, jealousy | Privacy respected or alibi verified |
| Gideon Rusk | Hostile, watchful, ashamed | Ledger provenance, trailer sighting, old plant | June funds son's treatment | Son's care separated from June or physical vehicle proof shown |
| Vera Bell | Dry humor, maternal steel | Vehicle sighting and time | Driver identity | Empathy about debt/loyalty or contradiction with register tape |
| Amos Bell | Defensive, profane, practical | Missing drug and June's farm visit | Illegal disposal and threat | Shown the batch match or offered narrow immunity |
| Rowan Pike | Soft-spoken, morally rigid | Ledger, survivor names, Hush Woman image | Celia meeting, archive hiding place | Survivor privacy protected or lawful search succeeds |
| Mae Orlov | Flirtatious, transactional, fearless | Affair, timing, coat condition | Extortion photo and room cleaning | Bargain, leverage, or evidence she cannot safely keep the photo |
| Silas Dredge | Courtly, macabre, lonely | Tool marks and 1912 history | Unauthorized human specimens | Treated as an expert instead of a monster |

## Portrait continuity

- **June Kessler:** white woman, 52, immaculate silver-blonde bob, pale gray eyes, cream wool civic coat with gold-threaded buttons, composed smile.
- **Celia Wren:** white/Latina woman, 38, dark wavy shoulder-length hair, intense brown eyes, burgundy leather jacket, radio headphones often around neck.
- **Hollis Cade:** Black man, 56, broad build, close-cropped graying hair, trimmed mustache, worn tan sheriff uniform.
- **Miriam Vale:** East Asian woman, 47, slender, chin-length black hair threaded with gray, round glasses, dark medical coat.
- **Naomi Venn:** Black woman, 32, lean, short natural curls, copper nose ring, oversized cardigan and band T-shirt.
- **Gideon Rusk:** white man, 61, heavy shoulders, scarred hands, gray beard, old canvas plant coat and knit cap.
- **Vera Bell:** white woman, 63, red-gray hair, strong lined face, green diner uniform and cat-eye glasses.
- **Amos Bell:** white man, 59, rangy veterinarian, windburned face, sandy-gray hair, farm jacket over faded scrubs.
- **Rowan Pike:** Latino man, 44, lean, tired dark eyes, black clerical shirt, collar, worn charcoal cardigan.
- **Mae Orlov:** Eastern European woman, 46, platinum hair with dark roots, crimson lipstick, leopard-print blouse under motel cardigan.
- **Silas Dredge:** white man, 50, tall and gaunt, swept-back black hair graying at temples, charcoal undertaker suit.

## Investigator portraits

- **Nora Mercer:** white Northern European woman, 36, state major-crimes investigator, athletic build, fair skin, gray-blue eyes, collar-length dark ash-blonde hair, dark oxblood field coat, observant and steady.
- **Isaac Ward:** white Northern European man, 39, forensic scene specialist, lean build, fair skin, blue-gray eyes, dark blond hair, short beard, navy work jacket, canvas evidence satchel, methodical gaze.
