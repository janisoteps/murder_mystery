# Season 1 scenes and pacing

> GAME-MASTER SPOILERS.

## Opening image

At 8:50 p.m., freezing rain taps the Sheriff-Coroner Annex. Celia Wren's body hangs upside down in the Saint Orra brine room, abdomen opened, throat hollowed, and ears plugged with metallic red wax. The player-facing briefing contains no correct theory of death.

Let the investigators choose their route. Never dump a location's full clue list on arrival. Give one strong sensory detail, establish visible NPCs, then let questions and declared searches determine what becomes evidence.

## Location scenes

### Sheriff-Coroner Annex

Present initially: Sheriff Hollis Cade and Dr. Miriam Vale.

- Hollis pushes a transient-killer theory and withholds the 1988 file.
- Miriam describes obvious trauma but calls the bolt wound “suspiciously dry” if treated as a colleague.
- A deliberate autopsy or microscopy action can reveal postmortem staging, xylazine, and Blackwater diatoms in stages.
- Hollis's dispatch log clears him of the murder but implicates him in protecting old files.

### Saint Orra Packing House

Present initially: Gideon Rusk.

- The body tableau and industrial horror should be described intensely but precisely.
- Gideon knows the Hush Woman pose and remembers the utility trailer, but fear makes him abrasive.
- Searching the loading bay yields tracks and blue fibers; reconstructing blood patterns proves the site is secondary.
- A reckless investigator can be knocked into a moving rail or locked in a brine room. The other can cut power or release the manual brake.

### WOHF Radio

Present initially: Naomi Venn.

- Naomi is furious, grieving, and terrified that Celia's private recordings will surface.
- Her on-air archive is an objective alibi.
- The erased confession reel requires careful repair or Naomi's cooperation.
- June may target Naomi here after 10:25 if Gideon is protected.

### Blackwater Pump House

Present initially: no one.

- The west basin has been scrubbed too recently; the air smells of chlorine, iron, and wet concrete.
- Access logs, missing blanket, matching water, and the button can establish the real scene.
- This is the preferred confrontation location after all three proof chains converge.

### Hollowfield Town Hall

Present initially: Mayor June Kessler. The municipal clerk and other unnamed staff have already gone home.

- June is calm, useful, and personally warm with investigators. She offers records that point toward Hollis.
- The coat is hidden in a locked wardrobe after the first direct question about her evening.
- Wax, falsified minutes, and the telephone record create escalating pressure.
- June must not confess before the accusation gate.

### Mercy Diner

Present initially: Vera Bell.

- Vera can place the civic car and trailer but protects June out of debt and loyalty.
- The 7:24 cash-register tape fixes the sighting time.
- Empathy succeeds faster than threats; threats make Vera call June.

### Bell Farm & Veterinary Barn

Present initially: Dr. Amos Bell.

- Amos's livestock violations and threat against Celia are a substantial red herring.
- The missing vial matches toxicology. Festival-camera film places June at the refrigerator.
- Mishandling a sedated boar can injure or trap an investigator; the partner can open the crush gate.

### St. Leda's Church Archive

Present initially: Father Rowan Pike.

- Rowan has the red ledger and the 1912 Hush Woman photograph.
- He lies because of confession, survivor privacy, and fear—not because he killed Celia.
- Respecting survivor dignity earns the ledger. A lawful search can also find it behind the loose confessional panel.

### Northline Motor Lodge

Present initially: Mae Orlov.

- Room 8 establishes the consensual affair between Celia and June. Adult sexual traces may be described when relevant, without turning the scene pornographic.
- Mae kept a Polaroid for extortion and erased the official guest ledger entry.
- The timestamp shows June's coat intact shortly before the murder.

### Dredge Funeral Home

Present initially: Silas Dredge.

- Silas is unsettling, knowledgeable, and innocent.
- Comparative tool work establishes that the mutilation was postmortem and done with a packing knife.
- His private Ada Orra collection makes him look guilty until its provenance is checked.

## Escalation clock

Use both story time and player progress. Never punish careful roleplay merely for taking real-world time.

1. **Unease:** after the first major proof, an anonymous caller plays three seconds of Celia choking underwater.
2. **Interference:** after the second proof chain begins, June removes her coat, orders files burned, and relocates if necessary. Reflect this in `peopleAtLocations` and `sceneFlags`.
3. **Second target, 10:25 p.m.:** Gideon dies in the scalding pit unless warned/protected. If protected, June attempts to abduct Naomi; this is preventable.
4. **Capture:** once three chains converge, June offers a final tape at Blackwater. One investigator may be trapped in the sluice inspection chamber. The other must restore power at the east panel, find the red wheel key, and open the flood gate manually. Failure causes injury and rising water, never death.
5. **Finale:** June can be arrested, cornered into confession, or rendered unable to flee. Evidence—not violence—determines the successful ending.

## Ending variants

- **Clean resolution:** three chains, rescue succeeds, at least one secondary target survives, and the 1988 truth is preserved with survivor privacy.
- **Pyrrhic resolution:** June is caught, but Gideon dies or survivors' private records are broadcast.
- **Unproven truth:** players identify June but lack one chain; she survives formal accusation and the campaign ends with a chilling epilogue, not a retroactive culprit change.
- **Wrong accusation:** show consequences, allow one recovery opportunity through a remaining decisive clue, and keep the fixed truth.
